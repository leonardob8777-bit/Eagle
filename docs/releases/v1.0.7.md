# Eagle 1.0.7 (102)

- Added Russian and Vietnamese to Settings and the welcome language selector, including system prompts and translated update notes.
- Completed Vietnamese coverage for recent controls and diagnostic messages; verified 83 templates preserve their inserted values.
- Wallpaper and passcode catalog names follow the selected language, with English fallback.
- Added likes, HOT ordering and online presence, with an introductory guide.
- Refreshed galleries, previews, Settings, notifications and Passcode actions.
- Saved PosterBoard UUID support lets the wallpaper workflow reuse previously prepared access.
- Kept Prepare available to iPads within the engine range and excluded M5 and unvalidated iPadOS 16 combinations.

## Compatibility and validation

Prepare range: iOS/iPadOS 17.0–18.7.1 and 26.0–26.0.1, excluding A19/M5.
The existing iPhone-only 16.7.2 exception remains. Dynamic Island requires compatible iPhone hardware.

Release build and package integrity, language dispatch, Vietnamese phrase/template coverage,
system localization resources, and 50 public/laboratory Prepare checks were verified.
This release has not been accepted on a physical iPad; individual iPad effects remain unverified.
Translation coverage checks do not replace review by a native Vietnamese speaker.

The IPA requires signing with your usual personal installation method.
Download: [Eagle-1.0.7-102.ipa](https://github.com/leonardob8777-bit/Eagle/releases/download/v1.0.7/Eagle-1.0.7-102.ipa).
